# What the narrator says in the schedule tour, in order. One line per key.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll plan a whole service in the Schedule, and run it from start to finish.
panel = The Schedule sits on the left. It's your service in order: songs, readings, videos, pictures and announcements, in one list.
count = The count at the top tells you how many items the service has.
add-from-tab = You can add something to the schedule from any tab. Here in the Bible tab, we'll find John 3:16.
add-button = Then click Add to Schedule. The verse joins the end of the service.
add-files = To add pictures, videos or presentations from your computer, click Add Files. You can also drag files straight from your desktop into the schedule.
label = Labels divide the service into parts, like Worship, Sermon and Announcements. Click Add Label.
label-name = Give it a name. We'll call this one Closing.
label-colors = Then pick its colors. Choose a text color and a background color, so each part of the service stands out at a glance.
label-edit = The label appears in the schedule in its own colors. To change its name or colors later, click Edit Label.
row-buttons = Each item has its own buttons. Let's go through them.
go-live-button = Go Live puts that item straight on the big screen.
reorder = The up and down arrows move an item one place at a time. You can also drag any item by its handle.
note = The note button lets you leave a message for whoever runs the service.
note-text = We'll write: Read slowly, then pause for prayer. The note stays with the item.
remove = And Remove takes an item out of the service.
preview = Click any item to open it in its tab and see it in the preview.
go-live = When it's time, click Go Live.
live = The reading is now on the projector.
double-click = You can also double-click any item to put it live in a single move.
undo = Changed your mind? Undo brings back what you just changed, and Redo does it again.
new = Now the buttons across the top. New starts a fresh schedule.
open = Open loads a schedule you saved before.
save = Save keeps this one as a schedule file on your computer. The first time, you choose where it goes and give it a name.
save-as = To keep a copy under a new name, use Save Schedule As in the File menu.
share = A saved schedule is a single file, so you can email it or share it, and your team can load it with Open.
clear = Clear Schedule empties the list in one click, and Undo can still bring it back.
autosave = Your schedule is also saved automatically as you work. If the computer restarts unexpectedly, ChurchPresenter offers to bring it back.
planning-center = If your church plans its services in Planning Center, this button imports a plan straight into the schedule, songs and all.
calendar = The Calendar Manager plans services weeks ahead, each with its own run of show, and can load one into the schedule automatically before the service starts.
export = From the Calendar Manager, you can also export a service's run of show as a PDF, to print or hand to your team.
zoom = The zoom buttons make the rows bigger or smaller.
density = And you can choose how much each row shows: Compact, Normal or Detailed.
options = Last, the Schedule Options menu. Open it to set up the panel the way you like.
options-buttons = Under Buttons, every toolbar button has a tick box. Untick the ones you never use, and they disappear from the toolbar. The item count and zoom controls can be hidden here too.
options-under-title = Show buttons under title moves the toolbar onto its own row, below the Schedule heading.
options-size = And Toolbar icon size makes the buttons small, medium or large.
outro = And that's the Schedule. Add anything from any tab, put it in order, and lead the whole service from one list.
