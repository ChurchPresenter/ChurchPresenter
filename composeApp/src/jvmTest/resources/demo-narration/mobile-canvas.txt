intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll draw on a tablet with the mobile app's Canvas, and put the drawing on the big screen.
why = A preacher can sketch a point during the sermon, a children's leader can draw the story as they tell it, and everyone in the room sees every stroke as it's drawn.
server = First, connect the tablet to your computer. On the computer, open Settings and go to the Server page.
start = Switch on the Companion Server. It lets phones and tablets on your church network connect to this computer.
qr = Click QR to show the connection code. It holds this computer's address, so nobody has to type it.
tablet-mode = The first time you open the mobile app on the tablet, it asks how you'll present. Choose Remote control, so the tablet drives ChurchPresenter on your computer.
scan = Scan the code with the tablet's camera, and the app fills in the address for you.
manual = If the camera isn't an option, open the app's Settings, go to Server, and type the computer's address and port. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.
connected = The tablet now shows that the server is connected, along with what this device is allowed to do.
open = Now, on the tablet, open the More tab and tap Canvas.
empty = A new canvas opens as a blank page, ready to draw on.
rail = Down the left side are the tools. From the top: Select, Lasso, Pen, Highlighter and Eraser, then Shapes, Line and Arrow, Text, Sticky Note and Image.
pen = With the Pen, just draw with your finger or a stylus.
stylus = For better accuracy, we recommend drawing with a stylus or a tablet pen rather than a finger, especially for handwriting.
pen-bar = The pen bar along the bottom picks the color, the thickness and the opacity.
pen-options = Tap the Pen again for more: a ballpoint, fountain, brush or pencil, smoothing, and pressure sensitivity for a stylus.
highlighter = The Highlighter marks things up without hiding what's underneath, like the line under the title.
eraser = The Eraser removes a whole stroke, just the part you rub out, or everything inside an area.
shapes = Shapes draws rectangles, ellipses, stars, speech bubbles and more, with a fill and a border of your choice.
snap = And with Snap Rough Drawings to Shapes switched on, a circle drawn by hand becomes a neat one.
line = Line and Arrow joins things together, straight, curved or with an elbow.
text = Text types a heading or a Bible reference, in the font and size you choose.
sticky = A sticky note holds a question or a reminder, in a handwriting font.
image = Image brings in a picture from your photo library or your files.
select = With Select, tap anything to move it, resize it or turn it, and the bar under it can bring it to the front, duplicate, lock or delete it.
layers = The Layers button lists everything on the page, top to bottom, to hide, lock or reorder.
pages = A canvas can have many pages. They're along the bottom right, and the plus button adds a new one.
saved = The canvas saves itself as you work, and the top of the screen says when it's saved.
go-live = When you're ready, tap Go Live.
ask = The computer asks before anything appears, just as it does for a song sent from the tablet. Once you allow the tablet for the session, it goes straight up.
live = The page is now on the big screen. Every stroke you add shows up there as you draw it.
end-live = The button now reads End Live. Tap it to take the drawing down.
cleared = The screen clears, and the tablet is ready to draw again.
step-down = If anything else goes live from the computer, the drawing steps aside.
go-live-again = Tap Go Live again on the tablet to bring it back.
more-menu = The menu at the top right can rename the canvas, duplicate or delete a page, or clear the page or the whole canvas. You can always undo.
outro = And that's the Canvas: draw on the tablet, and the whole room sees it.
