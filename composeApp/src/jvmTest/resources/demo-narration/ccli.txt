intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll look at the CCLI report: a record of every song and verse you put on screen.
why = If your church has a CCLI license, you report the songs you project. ChurchPresenter keeps that record for you, so there's nothing to write down.
automatic = There's nothing to turn on. Each time a song or a verse goes live, ChurchPresenter notes what it was and when.
go-live = Let's put a song on the screen. Click Go Live, and this use of the song is counted.
plays = In the Songs tab, the Plays column shows how many times each song has been used.
open = To see the full report, open the Help menu and choose CCLI Reports.
window = The report opens on this year so far. Everything in it comes from your own services.
presets = These buttons pick a quick range: the last three, six or twelve months, or all time.
year = The Year menu picks one calendar year.
dates = For any other range, set the From and To dates. Match them to the period CCLI asks you to report.
songs-chart = On the Songs tab, the chart on the left shows your most used songs.
songs-table = The table lists every song with its author, song book, CCLI number, and how many times it was used, with the first and last dates.
ccli-number = A dash means the song has no CCLI number yet. Add it in the song editor, in the CCLI Number field, and it shows up here.
songbook = This menu narrows the list to one song book.
bible = The Bible tab does the same for scripture, with the books you read from most.
activity = The Activity tab shows how busy you've been over time, with songs and verses side by side.
activity-cards = These cards add up the songs, the verses, and your busiest month.
remove = Hover over a song to remove it. That's handy when a song went live by mistake during a rehearsal.
export-csv = When it's time to report, click Export CCLI CSV and save the file.
csv-use = The file lists the title, author, CCLI number and times used for every song, ready for your CCLI report.
xls = Export XLS saves the same report as a spreadsheet, to share or print.
clear = Clear Statistics starts over from nothing. It asks first, because it can't be undone.
outro = And that's the CCLI report: every song counted for you, and ready to send when you need it.
