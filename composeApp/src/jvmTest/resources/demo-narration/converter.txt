intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll look at the Converter, which brings your songs and Bibles in from other programs, finds duplicate songs, and renames song files in bulk.
open = Open it from the Help menu, or press Control, Shift, K. On a Mac, it's Command, Shift, K.
tabs = The window has four tabs: Bibles, Songs, Duplicates and Rename.
songs-why = First, songs. If you're moving to ChurchPresenter from another program, you shouldn't have to leave your song library behind. Which software you use should be entirely your choice, and your hands shouldn't be tied by the songs you've already entered. The song converter isn't here to pull people away from other software. It's here so the songs your church has built up over the years come with you.
songs-formats = Pick the program you're coming from. It reads SongBeamer, OpenLP, OpenSong, FreeShow, Free Worship, EasySlides, Quelea, SoftProjector and VideoPsalm, and it can pull lyrics out of PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Keynote files.
songs-pick = For this tour, we'll convert a few songs from SongBeamer.
songs-select = Next, select the song files you want to bring in, or choose a whole folder of them at once.
songs-destination = Choose where the converted songs go. By default, they're saved next to the originals.
songs-preview = Preview shows each song as it will come out, so you can check it before converting.
songs-convert = Then click Convert, and the songs are ready for your library.
songs-request = Don't see your program? Open a feature request and attach a few sample songs exported from it. With a handful of examples, we'll try to build a converter for that program too.
bible-intro = The Bibles tab converts Zeh-fah-nia X M L Bible files into ChurchPresenter's own Bible format.
bible-licence = Just like downloading a Bible in Settings, licensing matters here. Bible translations are protected by copyright, and some can't be shown publicly, streamed or broadcast without a license. Only convert and display Bibles your church is licensed to use. If the file carries a copyright notice, the converter keeps it with the Bible.
bible-convert = Select the X M L files you want to convert, check them in the preview, and then click Convert.
bible-fix = Fix Known Bible Verses corrects verses known to be incomplete in Bibles you've already converted.
dupes-intro = Over the years, a library can collect the same song more than once, especially after merging collections. The Duplicate Finder finds them.
dupes-folder = Start by choosing the folder where your songs are kept.
dupes-passes = It scans in three passes: by song number, by title, and by how similar the lyrics are.
dupes-threshold = The similarity threshold sets how close two songs' lyrics must be to count as the same song.
dupes-clean = It can also find letters that look alike but aren't, like a Latin letter hiding in Cyrillic text, and invisible control characters that break searching.
dupes-scan = When you're ready, click Scan for Duplicates to start the search.
dupes-results = Matches are grouped, each with the reason it matched and how similar the songs are.
dupes-keep = Pick a Keep Folder, and every copy outside it is marked for deletion automatically. Or you can mark files for deletion yourself.
dupes-delete = Then delete the copies you've marked. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is removed.
dupes-again = It's worth scanning again after deleting, since that can catch duplicates the first pass missed.
rename-intro = Finally, Bulk Rename tidies up the names of your song files.
rename-numbers = The Strip leading numbers option turns a name like zero one one one, dash, Amazing Grace into just Amazing Grace.
rename-first-line = Or you can rename each song after the first line of its first verse.
rename-case = Letter case can make every name Title Case, Sentence case, lowercase or uppercase.
rename-preview = The preview shows every new name before anything changes, and flags any that would clash.
rename-go = When everything looks right, click Rename to rename all of them at once.
outro = And that's the Converter. Bring your library with you, clean up duplicates, and keep your song files tidy.
