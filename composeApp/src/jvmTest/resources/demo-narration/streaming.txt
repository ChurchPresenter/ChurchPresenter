intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll send your lyrics and scripture to your livestream, straight from ChurchPresenter.
why = The projector in the room isn't the only screen. Your stream can show the same words, often as a lower third over the camera.
ways = ChurchPresenter has four ways to get them there: a Browser Source for OBS, NDI, OMT, and Blackmagic DeckLink cards.
settings = They're all set up in one place. Open Settings, and go to the Projection page.
browser = Start with Browser Source Outputs. It's the simplest way into OBS, and there's nothing extra to install.
browser-add = Click Add Output to make one.
browser-name = Give it a name, like Stream, so you know what it's for.
browser-profile = Pick the profile it shows. A Lower Third profile puts the words along the bottom, over your camera.
browser-server = Browser Sources come from the companion server, so make sure it's turned on in the Server settings.
browser-copy = Then click Copy URL for Transparent Background. Everything behind the words stays see-through.
browser-obs = In OBS, add a Browser Source, and paste it in. Your lyrics now appear on the stream.
ndi = NDI sends the picture over your network, to OBS, vMix or a video switcher.
ndi-runtime = NDI needs the free NDI Runtime installed on this computer. Once it is, the card says it's ready.
ndi-add = Add an output, and give it a name. That's the name your switcher sees on the network.
ndi-mode = For a lower third, choose Alpha. It carries real transparency, so the words arrive already keyed.
ndi-receivers = The card also shows how many receivers are watching it.
omt = OMT works the same way. It's the open-source alternative to NDI, and it comes built into ChurchPresenter, so there's nothing to install.
decklink = And if you have a Blackmagic DeckLink card, it shows up in Screen Assignment like another screen, so an output can go straight out over SDI or HDMI.
outro = And that's streaming outputs: the words in the room and on the stream, from one place.
