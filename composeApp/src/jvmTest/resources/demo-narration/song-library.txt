intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll look at the Song Library Manager, where you can see and edit every song in your library at once.
why = One song at a time is fine on a Sunday. But renumbering a song book, filling in a missing composer, or moving a set of songs into a new book means opening each one. The Song Library Manager does that work in one screen.
open = Open it from the Help menu, or press Control, Shift, L. On a Mac, it's Command, Shift, L.
window = Every song in your songs folder is listed here, with its number, title, song book, author, composer, tune and CCLI number. The header shows how many songs and song books you have.
search = Search by title, author or number, and the list narrows as you type.
books = The song book menu shows one book at a time, or songs that aren't in any book.
sort = Click a column heading to sort by it. Click it again to reverse the order.
columns = Columns lets you show or hide what's in the grid, like the second and third language titles.
duration = The Duration column shows how long each song usually stays on screen, measured from your real services.
edit-cell = To change a field, click it and type. Enter keeps the change, and Escape throws it away.
book-cell = The song book field is a menu of your books, so a typo can never create a new one by accident.
pending = Nothing is written yet. Your changes are counted at the bottom, and you can Revert them, or click Save Changes to write them to the library.
select = Tick songs to select them. Shift-click selects a range, and Control-click, or Command-click on a Mac, picks songs one at a time.
batch = With songs selected, choose Batch Edit.
batch-fields = Tick each field you want to change, like the author or the song book, and type the new value. Unticked fields stay as they are. Then apply it to every selected song at once.
new-book = New Song Book makes a new collection, like Christmas twenty twenty-six. If songs are selected, you can file them under it straight away.
edit-song = The pencil on each song opens the same Edit Song window the Songs tab uses, so a song is edited in one place wherever you start.
new-song = New Song adds a song to the library.
delete = Delete removes the selected songs' files from your library folder. You'll be asked first, because it can't be undone.
problems = A flashing exclamation mark beside a song means its languages need attention. Sections might not line up, or a language might be missing its title or its lyrics. Hover over it to see exactly what's wrong.
compare = To fix it, click Compare translations, the arrows at the end of the song, which turn amber when sections don't line up. Each language is shown side by side, section by section, with every problem marked.
compare-problem = Here, verse two has four lines in English but only three in Spanish, so it's marked Line count differs. Add the missing line, and it lines up.
compare-show = The chips at the top choose which languages are shown. Click one to hide that translation, and click it again to bring it back.
compare-only = Only problems hides the sections that already line up, so you can go straight to what needs fixing.
outro = And that's the Song Library Manager: your whole library in one screen, edited many songs at a time.
