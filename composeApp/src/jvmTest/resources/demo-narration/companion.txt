intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll connect ChurchPresenter to Bitfocus Companion, so you can run your service from a Stream Deck, and see your Stream Deck's buttons right inside the app.
what = Companion is a free app that runs the buttons on an Elgato Stream Deck. Each button can do something in another program, like your video switcher, your lights, or ChurchPresenter.
two-ways = ChurchPresenter works with Companion in two ways. Your Stream Deck can control ChurchPresenter, and ChurchPresenter can show Companion's buttons on its own screen.
server = First, the Stream Deck side. Open Settings, and go to the Server page.
server-on = Make sure the companion server is running. That's what lets Companion reach ChurchPresenter.
triggers = Lower third triggers lists every lower third in your folder, each with buttons that copy its trigger for Companion.
takedown = Hide Lower Third and Clear Display copy the addresses that take things off the screen.
go-live = Click Go Live beside Welcome, and its trigger is copied, ready to paste into Companion.
plus-key = Plus Key copies one that also turns on the key on your ATEM switcher, if you have one.
companion-connection = Now, in Companion, add a Generic HTTP Requests connection.
companion-button = Pick a Stream Deck button, and give it an HTTP POST action.
companion-paste = Paste what you copied into the URL box.
press = Now that Stream Deck button puts Welcome on the screen, without anyone touching the computer.
satellite = Now the other way around. Go to the Companion Satellite page in Settings.
host = Type the address of the computer that runs Companion.
grid = Set how many rows and columns of buttons to show, to match your Stream Deck.
show-in = Choose where the buttons appear: in their own tab, or in the left or right sidebar.
connect = Click Connect, and Companion sees ChurchPresenter as one more Stream Deck.
companion-tab = Open the Companion tab, and there are your buttons, with the same labels and colors as on the Stream Deck.
click = Click a button, and it does exactly what the real button does.
sidebar-open = To put the buttons in a sidebar, open the Companion Satellite page again.
sidebar-tick = Tick Right sidebar. You can tick Left sidebar too, or both.
sidebar-grid = A sidebar is narrow, so give it a smaller grid, like two rows of four.
sidebar = Close Settings, and there they are, right beside the live preview, ready during the service.
page = Which page of buttons it shows is set in Companion itself, under Surfaces.
outro = And that's Companion: your Stream Deck and ChurchPresenter, working together.
