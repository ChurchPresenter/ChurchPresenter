# What the narrator says in the tablet remote tour, in order. One line per key.
# Every line plays over what it names, and every tap from one screen to the next is shown.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll change the slides from a tablet, so whoever is speaking can run their own presentation from the pulpit.
tab = On the church computer, open the Presentation tab.
recent = Double-click a recent file to load its slides. This one is a PowerPoint, with speaker notes on every slide.
remote-open = Then click Remote Control, at the top of the slides.
enable = Switch on Enable Remote Control.
qr = The QR code holds the remote's address. Scan it with the tablet's camera.
opens = The remote opens in the tablet's web browser, so there's no app to install. The first time, it waits for the computer to say yes.
approve = On the computer, ChurchPresenter asks whether this tablet may control the slides.
allow = Click Allow for Session, and the tablet can change the slides until ChurchPresenter closes.
page = Now the tablet shows the slide that's on screen, with the next slide beside it, and every slide underneath.
go-live = On the computer, click Go Live, so the slides are on the big screen.
forward = On the tablet, tap Forward.
notes = Across the top are the speaker notes, straight from the PowerPoint or Keynote file. They're only on the tablet, never on the big screen.
forward-live = The next slide is on the big screen at once.
jump = To jump straight to any slide, tap it in the list underneath. Let's tap slide five.
jump-live = Slide five goes up on the big screen.
backward = Tap Backward to go back a slide.
backward-live = And the big screen goes back with it.
upload = The speaker can bring their own slides, too. On the tablet, tap Upload.
pick = The tablet's file picker opens. Choose the PowerPoint or Keynote file. Let's tap Easter Sunday.
uploaded = The file goes straight to the church computer, and moments later the tablet shows its slides, notes and all.
upload-desk = On the computer, the new presentation opens in the Presentation tab, ready to go live.
outro = And that's the tablet remote: the slides and the notes in the speaker's hand, and their own presentations sent straight from the tablet.
