# What the narrator says in the overview tour, in order. One line per key.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll take a quick look around the main window, so you know where everything is before your first service.
layout = The window has three parts. Your schedule is on the left, the tabs are in the middle, and on the right, you can see exactly what's on every screen.
schedule = The schedule is your service in order: songs, Bible readings, videos and announcements, ready to go one after another. When you need more room, collapse it with this arrow.
tabs = Across the top are the tabs. Each one shows a different kind of content on the big screen. Let's open each one.
tabs-word = The Bible tab finds any passage in seconds, and the Songs tab holds your whole song library.
tabs-media = Pictures, Presentation and Media show your photos, your slides, and your videos and music.
tabs-more = Lower Third introduces speakers with a name banner, and Announcements shows messages, countdowns and clocks.
tabs-rest = Web shows any web page, Canvas lets you design your own screens, and Dictionary looks up the original Hebrew and Greek words.
tab-visibility = You don't have to keep every tab. Open Tab Visibility, and untick the tabs your church doesn't use, and they disappear from the bar. Two tabs, Q and A and live captions, start out hidden. Tick them here whenever you need them.
columns = Most of the window can be resized, too. Drag the edge of a panel or a column to give each part the room you need.
pattern = Every tab works the same way. Choose what you want to show, and it appears in the preview first, so you can check it before anyone else sees it.
go-live = Then click Go Live, and it's sent to the projector. Let's put John three sixteen on the screen.
live = And there it is, on the big screen.
add-to-schedule = Next to Go Live, every tab has Add to Schedule, so you can plan the whole service ahead of time.
preview = On the right is a live preview of every screen, so you always know what the congregation is seeing.
three-screens = Here we have three screens: the projector at full screen, a second screen that shows only lower thirds, and a stage monitor for the people up front, with the time, what's next and their notes.
screen = Each preview is labeled with its screen, and with its profile. A profile is the look of that screen: its fonts, colors and layout.
swap = To give a screen a different look, swap its profile right here.
lock = The lock keeps a screen on the tab it's showing, so a stage display or a second room isn't changed by what you do in the other tabs.
clear = When you're finished, Clear Display takes everything off the screen.
displays = Toggle Presenter Displays turns the projector windows on and off.
preview-settings = And Preview Settings choose which screens appear here, and how they're laid out.
background = Back at the top, the Background button sets what sits behind your words, from a solid color to a picture or a video.
settings = And Settings is where you set up your screens and profiles, add Bibles, turn on the companion server for phones and remote control, and much more.
storage = One setting worth knowing about early is on the System page: the folders where your Bibles and songs are kept. They're stored outside the app, so your library stays safe every time you update ChurchPresenter, and you can point them at a shared or backed-up folder.
remote = ChurchPresenter also has apps for Android and iOS. Once the companion server is on, you can give access to the people you trust, like your youth leader, your worship team or your pastor, and they connect from their own phones and tablets.
remote-what = From there, they can see the schedule, add songs and verses to it, and help you run the service remotely, such as moving through the sermon slides from the pulpit. And you stay in charge. When a device asks to change something, a prompt like this one appears. Allow it once, trust that device for the rest of the service or for good, or deny it.
standalone = The mobile app can even present on its own, with no computer at all, sending your songs and Bibles to a TV.
planning = There are planning tools too. If your church uses Planning Center, this button opens the import. Choose a service plan, and its songs, readings and announcements come straight into the schedule. Songs already in your library are matched, and new ones can be added right here.
calendar = The Calendar Manager plans services weeks ahead. Each service has its own run of show, which can load into the schedule automatically before the service starts, or be exported as a PDF for your team.
song-library = The Song Library Manager, in the Help menu, shows your whole song library in one grid. Edit titles, authors and song books for many songs at once, and check that every translation of a song has the same sections.
converter = And the converter brings whole libraries over from other programs, like OpenLP, SongBeamer, FreeShow, OpenSong and EasySlides, and even lifts lyrics out of PDF, PowerPoint and Word files, so nobody has to retype a song.
more = There's much more than we can show in one video, so here are a few highlights.
more-languages = ChurchPresenter runs in thirty-five languages, with twelve themes to suit your booth, and it all works offline, so you don't need the internet on Sunday morning.
more-bible = Show up to six Bible translations at once, download more than fifteen hundred translations from inside the app, and even let ChurchPresenter follow the preacher, spotting Bible references in live speech and bringing up the verse on its own.
more-outputs = Every screen can have its own look, whether it's a projector, a TV or a DeckLink device, and you can send your output over the network with NDI, or into OBS and vMix as a browser source.
more-stage = The stage monitor gives speakers and musicians the time, what's coming next, notes and chord charts, laid out in up to five zones.
more-integrations = It works with Blackmagic ATEM switchers, changes OBS scenes for you, and connects to Bitfocus Companion and Stream Deck.
more-reporting = ChurchPresenter also counts every song and verse you show. The CCLI report lists your most used songs for any date range, and exports a file ready for your CCLI license reporting.
more-link = And you can link a second copy of ChurchPresenter, in an overflow room or at another campus, so it shows whatever you put live.
wick = Finally, meet Wick, the built-in helper. Turn it on from the Help menu, then just type what you want, like show John three sixteen, or where do I add a song, and Wick does it or points you to the right button. It always asks before changing anything, and it runs right on your own computer.
outro = And that's ChurchPresenter at a glance. Watch the other videos in this series to learn each tab in detail.
