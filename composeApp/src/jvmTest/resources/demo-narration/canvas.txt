# What the narrator says in the canvas tour, in order. Edit freely: one line per key.
# Write it the way it should sound -- full sentences; numbers as words where it matters.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll look at the Canvas tab, where you can design your own screens by layering pictures, text, video, live cameras and shapes.
open-tab = Let's open the Canvas tab.
scenes = Everything you design here is called a scene. Your scenes are listed on the left, and each one is a complete screen, ready to show.
switch = Click a scene to open it. Its layers appear on the canvas in the middle, exactly as they'll look on the projector.
rename = Every scene has its own buttons. The pencil renames it, so it's easy to find later.
duplicate = The copy button makes a duplicate, which is handy when you want a variation of a scene you already have.
remove = And the cross deletes a scene you no longer need.
new-scene = Let's build a new scene from scratch. Click New.
canvas-size = The canvas size matches your projector, but you can change it here, for example for a wider screen or a stage display.
add-image = Now let's add some layers. Click the plus button under Sources, and choose Image.
image-props = On the right are the settings for the selected layer. Click Browse to choose a photo or your church logo, then decide whether it should fit inside the screen, fill it, or stretch to the edges.
add-color = Next, a Color layer, which is perfect for a background.
color-props = Pick a single solid color, or switch on Gradient to blend two colors together, at any angle you like.
add-video = A Video layer plays a video file as part of your scene.
video-props = Choose the file, then set it to loop, and choose how loud it should be.
add-text = Now let's add some text.
text-edit = Type your words, and they appear on the canvas straight away.
text-font = Choose any font on this computer, and set its size.
text-styles = These buttons make the words bold, italic, underlined or crossed out, and the last one adds an outline around the letters, so they stand out on any background.
text-align = Then choose how the text lines up, left, center or right, and whether it sits at the top, the middle or the bottom of its box.
text-color = Pick the font color, and if you like, a background color behind the words, or leave it transparent.
text-spacing = You can also spread the letters apart with letter spacing, or bend the line into a gentle curve.
add-clock = A Clock and Timer layer shows the time, or counts down to the start of the service.
clock-props = Choose twelve or twenty four hour time, and whether to show the hours and the seconds, along with the font, size and colors.
add-qr = A QR Code layer is something people can scan with their phone camera.
qr-props = Choose what the code opens, such as a web link to your giving page, your Wi-Fi details, a phone number, an email, or a contact card. You can also set its colors and how sturdy the code is.
add-browser = A Browser layer shows a live web page right inside your scene.
browser-props = Type the web address, choose its size, and you can even add your own styling with custom CSS.
add-bible = And a Bible layer shows a verse, styled to match the rest of your scene.
bible-props = Choose the Bible version, the book, and the start and end verses, then style the verse text and the reference separately.
live-sources = You can also bring in live video. Add a camera, capture another screen on this computer, or receive video over your network with NDI or OMT.
layers = Every layer in the list has its own buttons. The eye shows or hides it.
lock = The lock keeps it from being moved by accident.
order = The arrows move a layer forward or backward, so you can choose what sits on top.
delete = And the delete button removes a layer you don't need.
shapes = Above the canvas are the drawing tools. You can draw rectangles, ellipses, lines, arrows, or draw freehand.
draw = Pick a tool and drag on the canvas to draw.
shape-props = Every shape has its own fill color, stroke color and stroke width, and can use a gradient too.
select = Switch back to the Select tool to move and resize anything on the canvas.
back-welcome = Now let's go back to our finished Welcome scene.
go-live = When your scene is ready, click Go Live, and it's sent to the projector.
live = And there it is, the Welcome scene on the big screen.
preset = If you use a scene often, save it as a preset. A preset is a named shortcut that you can add to any future service in one click.
schedule = And to show it at just the right moment, click Add to Schedule.
outro = And that's the Canvas tab. Build a scene from pictures, text, video, live feeds and shapes, save it, and put it on the big screen whenever you need it.
