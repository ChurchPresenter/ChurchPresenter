# What the narrator says in the songs tour, in order. Edit freely: one line per key.
# Write it the way it should sound -- full sentences; numbers as words where it matters.

the-songs-tab = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll explore the Songs tab, where you can find any song in your library, show its verses, and lead worship right from the keyboard.
open-tab = Let's open the Songs tab. Your entire song library is listed here.
search-by-title = To find a song, just start typing its title. The list narrows down with every letter.
or-by-a-line-of-the-lyrics = Can't remember the title? Type a line from the lyrics instead. Each match even tells you which verse it was found in.
clear-the-search = Clear the search to bring back the whole library.
pick-a-song-book = If your library has several song books, you can show just one of them. Open the Song Book list.
gospel-songs = We'll pick Gospel Songs, and now only those songs are listed.
back-to-every-book = To see everything again, open the list once more.
all-song-books = And choose All Song Books.
choose-how-the-search-matches = The Filter list controls how your search matches. You can choose Contains, Starts With, or Exact Match.
starts-with = Starts With is handy when you know how a title begins.
sort-the-list = You can sort the list by clicking any column heading. Click it again to reverse the order.
click-a-song = Now let's choose a song. Click Amazing Grace, and its verses appear on the right.
click-a-verse = Click the first verse. It shows up in the preview, ready to go.
sends-the-verse-to-the-projector = Then click Go Live to send it to the projector.
verse-1-of-amazing-grace = The first verse of Amazing Grace is now on the big screen.
next-verse = During worship, you don't need the mouse at all. Press the down arrow to move to the next verse, and the up arrow to go back.
verse-2 = Verse two is live, straight from the keyboard.
double-click-a-verse = You can also double-click any verse to put it live in a single move.
verse-3-live = And now verse three is on the screen.
star-a-song = Songs you use often can be starred. Your favorites stay pinned in their own panel, so they're always close at hand.
right-click-a-song-for-more = Right-click any song for more options. You can go live, add it to the schedule, mark it as a favorite, edit it, or delete it.
add-to-schedule = Let's add It Is Well With My Soul to the schedule.
added-to-your-schedule = There it is, at the end of your service on the left.
back-to-live = Notice the red Back to Live bar. It appears whenever you browse away from the song that's on screen. Click it to jump straight back.
choose-the-columns = You can also choose which columns the list shows, such as the song number, the author, or how often a song has been played.
edit-a-song = To change a song, click Edit. You can update its lyrics, add chords and transpose them, include translations, and give it its own look.
write-a-new-song = And to add something new, click the plus button to write a new song, or import one.
that-s-the-songs-tab = And that's the Songs tab. Search, pick a verse, go live, and lead the rest of the song from your keyboard.
