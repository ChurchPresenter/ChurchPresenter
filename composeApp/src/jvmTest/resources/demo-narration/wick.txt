# What the narrator says in the Wick tour, in order. One line per key.
# Every line plays over what it names, and every click from one step to the next is shown.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll meet Wick, the little helper that lives in the corner of the app.
start = Wick is off until you start it. Open the Help menu at the top of the screen, and choose Show Helper.
hello = Wick lights up and introduces itself. It's a small helper bot, not an AI chatbot. It knows a set list of tasks, and it works without the internet.
next-1 = Click Next.
ask = You can type a short request in plain words, or try one of these. Let's click How do I add a song?
reply = Wick tells you what it will do, before it does anything.
next-2 = Click Next again.
quiet = Wick stays out of the way. It goes quiet while something is live, and new tips wait as a small badge on its button.
finish = Click Let's go.
lamp = Now Wick lives as a little lamp in the bottom right corner of the window.
open = Click the lamp to open Wick.
greeting = Wick opens with a tip of the day for the tab you're on. Previous and Next tip go through the others.
type = Type what you'd like in the box at the bottom, in plain words. Let's ask, where is the schedule?
spotlight = Wick shines a spotlight on the schedule, down the left of the window, and says what it's for.
request = Wick can do simple jobs, too. Let's type, make the song background blue.
confirm = Anything that changes a setting, Wick asks about first. Click Do it.
done = Wick makes the change, and offers Undo, in case you change your mind.
display-type = Wick can also set up your screens. Type, set up my screens.
display = Wick lists the screens connected to this computer. Click the one your congregation sees.
display-confirm = Wick checks with you before it changes anything. Click Do it.
test = Wick puts a big number on that screen. If you can see it, click Yes, I see it.
all-set = All set. Whatever you put live now shows on that screen.
close = When you're done, click the lamp again to close Wick.
bible-tab = Now, let's put a verse on the screen. Click the Bible tab.
pick-verse = Click a verse to choose it.
quiet-live = Then click Go Live.
still = While anything is live, Wick holds still and stays quiet, so it never pops up in the middle of a service.
outro = And that's Wick: a helper in the corner, there when you need it, and quiet when you don't.
