# What the narrator says in the Calendar Manager tour, in order. One line per key.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll plan services weeks ahead with the Calendar Manager, automate them with cues, and watch a service load into the schedule on its own.
open = Open the Calendar Manager from the Help menu, or with the calendar button on the Schedule.
month = Every planned service sits on a month view. Click a day to see what's planned, and its run of show opens on the right.
settings = First, the Calendar settings. They apply to every service.
sections = Under Sections, define names like Worship, Message and Response once, and use them in every run of show.
defaults = Under Defaults, choose the start time new services get, the length to plan for a song or a sermon until a real one is measured, and whether times show in twelve or twenty-four hour format.
auto-load = Here's one of the most useful settings. Turn on Load the service automatically, and choose how far ahead. Each service is put into the Schedule on its own, a few minutes before its first item, so it's ready when you arrive.
export-settings = The Export page sets the letterhead for your printed run of show: your church logo, name and address, and which sections are for staff only.
add-service = Now let's add a service. Click Add service.
service-details = Give it a date, a name and a start time, and choose its type: a Sunday service, midweek prayer, or a special event.
start-from = Start it blank, from a saved template, or as a copy of an earlier service.
add-items = Now fill in the run of show. Click Add, and choose from Songs, Bible, Section, Presets and Ministry.
add-song = Search your song library, and add a song with one click.
add-bible = Type a reference, like John three sixteen, or browse to a book and chapter and tap the verses you want.
add-section = Sections divide the service into parts, with the names you set up earlier.
add-preset = Presets are the shortcuts you saved on the other tabs: a welcome slideshow, an announcement loop, a countdown, or a video.
add-ministry = And Ministry is for what happens up front without a screen: the choir, a violin solo, a testimony. It's on the plan and the printout, and it's timed, but it never goes on the outputs.
durations = Every row has a length. Click it and type, like four thirty. ChurchPresenter also remembers how long each song usually runs here, and offers that time beside it.
reorder = Drag any row to change the order, or use the arrows to move it one place at a time.
times = Each row shows the time it's expected to start, worked out from the service start and the lengths before it.
timing = Now the automation. Click a row to open its timing.
starts = Starts sets how it begins. Cued waits for you to put it on screen. After previous goes live as the row before it ends. Or give it its own time, such as sixty minutes before the service, for a welcome loop that runs while people arrive.
runs = Runs sets how long it plays: its own length, or a time you choose.
repeats = Repeats plays it once, a set number of times, or in a loop until something else goes live.
at-end = And At end decides what happens next. Hold keeps it on screen, Next item puts the next row up on its own, and Blank clears the screens.
example = So a countdown can start sixty minutes before, loop until the service begins, then hand over to the first song, all on its own.
cues = Rows that start on their own are cues. They're armed by default. Turn one off, skip it, or fire it now by hand.
run-clock = The run clock steps the service forward five minutes at a time, so you can see exactly which cues would have fired, before Sunday ever comes.
repeat = Most churches plan the same service every week. Click Copy to paste this service onto another date, or repeat it weekly, every two weeks or monthly, as many times as you need.
template = Save the run of show as a template, and every new service can start from it.
export = Export PDF prints the run of show. The public copy is an order of service for the congregation. The staff copy has every time, length and cue.
load = When it's time, click Load into Schedule, or let the automatic load do it for you.
schedule = In the Schedule, every row arrives in order, with its start time and what it will do: starts on its own, loops until cued, plays twice, then advances. Ministry rows aren't loaded, but their time is counted, so the clock stays right.
during = During the service, cues fire on their own. A small card tells you each one went, and if something else was live at that moment, the cue is skipped, so it never interrupts you.
ahead = The schedule also shows whether you're ahead of plan or behind it, and any changes you make there can be saved back to the calendar.
outro = And that's the Calendar Manager. Plan weeks ahead, automate the timing, print the run of show, and let each service load itself before you arrive.
