# What the narrator says in the Calendar Manager tour, in order. One line per key.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll plan services days and weeks ahead with the Calendar Manager, set the pre-service loop and countdown to run by themselves, and let your team add to the plan from their phones.
why = Planning used to happen somewhere else, in a shared document, a group chat, or a printout, and someone typed it all in on Sunday morning. The Calendar Manager brings that planning into the app.
open = Open it from the Help menu, or with the Calendar Manager button on the Schedule toolbar. That button starts out hidden, so switch it on once in Schedule Options, under Buttons.
window = The calendar opens in its own window. The month is on the left, with each service marked in the color of its type: Sunday service, midweek prayer, or special event. The day's services are on the right, and the run of show, the order of everything that happens, is underneath.
saved = There's no Save button. Everything is saved as you go.
add-service = Let's plan a service. Click a day, then Add service.
service-details = Give it a name, like Sunday Morning, a start time, and a type.
start-from = Under Start from, choose a blank service, one of your templates, or a copy of an earlier service.
add-items = Now build the run of show. Click Add song, verse or section. The picker has five tabs.
add-song = Songs searches your library by title or number. Click one to add it.
add-bible = Bible takes a reference, like John three sixteen to seventeen, or you can browse to it.
add-section = Section adds colored headings, like Pre-Service, Worship, Word, Response and Closing, that divide the service into parts.
add-ministry = Ministry is for the parts people bring, with who's doing them: a special song, a violin piece, a poem, or a testimony. They're planned and timed like everything else, but they never appear on the screens.
add-preset = And Presets are reusable pieces of a service.
presets-how = To make a preset, set an item up on its own tab, such as Pictures, Presentation, Media, Announcements or Canvas, and click Save preset, next to Add to Schedule. Give it a name like Sunday announcements, and it's ready to drop into any service.
durations = Each row has a length. Once a song has been used in a few services, the calendar suggests how long it usually runs, based on how long it actually took.
lay-out = Then one click in the header times every row from the first, each starting when the one before it ends, so you can see at a glance whether the service fits its slot.
reorder = Drag any row to change the order, or move it with the arrows.
attention = If a row needs attention, such as a song that was deleted, a media file that moved, or a Bible reference that doesn't exist, the calendar flags it before Sunday, and clicking the warning takes you straight to the fix.
loop-intro = Now for the best part, a pre-service loop that runs by itself. We'll loop the announcement slideshow from thirty minutes before the service, and let a countdown take over for the last five.
timing = Click the slideshow row to open its timing panel. Every row has four settings.
starts = Starts sets when it begins: Cued waits for you, After previous follows the row before it, On time starts with the service, or a number of minutes before the service, or a clock time. We'll set it to minus thirty.
runs = Runs is the item's own length, or a length you set.
repeats = Repeats plays it once, a number of times, or in a loop. We'll choose Loop, so it keeps playing until something else goes live.
at-end = And At end holds the last frame, moves to the next item on its own, or blanks every output.
timing-summary = Underneath, the panel sums it all up in plain words: starts on its own at nine thirty, loops until cued, then holds.
countdown = Then add the countdown preset after it, and set it to start at minus five.
post-service = A post-service loop works the same way. Add it at the end, and set it to start after previous, or at a clock time.
armed = Finally, make sure the service is Armed. This is the switch that turns its timed cues on.
when-fire = Timed rows fire only when the service is in the Schedule tab and armed. And if something else is already live when a cue comes due, say the worship team is still playing, ChurchPresenter won't cut them off. It skips the cue and shows a note, so you can start it by hand if it should still go.
run-clock = To check your timing ahead of time, the Run clock steps the service clock forward five minutes at a time, and shows which cues would have fired.
repeat = Most weeks look alike, so you don't have to build every Sunday from scratch. Click Copy in the run of show header, choose Repeat, once, weekly, every two weeks or monthly, pick where the first copy goes and how many times, and create them all at once. Monthly repeats keep the weekday, so the third Sunday stays the third Sunday.
series = Each copy is its own service, so you can change one week's songs without touching the others. When a change should reach every week, choose All in series. And for an empty day, Copy last starts from the previous service.
template = When a service works, save it as a template. Choose whether to keep just the sections, or the items too. Next time, pick it under Start from, and the whole structure is there, timings and presets included. Templates are managed in Calendar settings.
load = On the day, select the service and click Load into Schedule. If the Schedule already has items, replace them, or add the plan to the end.
auto-load = Or skip even that step. Turn on Load the service automatically in Calendar settings, and each service loads into the Schedule a few minutes before its first item, five by default, and up to two hours, even with the calendar closed.
schedule = The service loads into the Schedule, every row in order, timings and all, ready to go.
schedule-times = Each row now shows its length, like five minutes, and the time it's expected to go live. A time in bold color means the row starts on its own at that time. A dimmer time is an estimate, worked out from the rows before it.
schedule-badges = Underneath, a row says what it will do: Loops, or Plays twice, and what happens after it, then next item, or then blank.
schedule-cues = As the service runs, each cue shows when it fired, or that it was skipped because something else was live, so you can start it by hand if it should still go.
schedule-drift = And the row that's live shows whether you're ahead of plan, behind it, or on plan, so you always know where the service stands.
schedule-notice = With automatic loading on, the Schedule shows a notice before the service, like Loading Sunday Morning in ten minutes, with a Load now button if you want it sooner. With an armed pre-service loop, your screens go from blank to announcements with nobody touching the computer.
pdf = Need a paper copy for the band or the pastor? Export PDF prints the run of show, ministry moments included. The public copy is an order of service for the congregation, and the staff copy has every time and cue. Your logo, church name and address go on the letterhead, set in Calendar settings.
phones = Sunday's plan rarely comes from one person. With calendar sync, your worship leader, your pastor and whoever runs the announcements can all work on it from the ChurchPresenter mobile app, from home, during the week, even while the church computer is switched off.
sync-on = To turn it on, open Settings, then Server, and find Calendar on phones and tablets. Switch on Sync the calendar with phones. The same switch is in the calendar's own settings.
relay = Sync goes through ChurchPresenter's relay over the internet, so phones don't need to be on the church WiFi. No account is needed, and the church computer always stays the source of truth.
secure = And your calendar stays private, to everyone, including us. It's encrypted on the church computer before it leaves, and it stays encrypted on the relay. The key is created on your computer and handed to each phone directly through the QR code. It never passes through the relay, so nobody else can read your services, not the ChurchPresenter team, and not anyone who got into the server.
invite = To add a phone, click Invite a phone. A QR code appears, and its owner scans it with the ChurchPresenter app, from anywhere. A phone already on the church WiFi can also ask to join. You'll be asked to allow it here, and both screens show the same code, so you know it's the right device.
enrolled = Every enrolled phone is listed here, with a Revoke button, for when a volunteer moves on.
mobile = From the mobile app, your team can see the month, plan services, and build the run of show from your church's own song library and Bible.
sync-timing = Changes reach the church computer every five minutes while it's open, when ChurchPresenter starts, and whenever you press Sync now. If two people edit the same service, the newer edit wins, and an old copy can never overwrite a newer one. Presets and templates are made on the computer, and loading the service into the Schedule happens there too.
getting-started = To get started, save your announcement slideshow and countdown as presets. Build one Sunday with a pre-service loop, check it with the run clock, and save it as a template. Copy it to the next few weeks, then invite your worship leader's phone to fill in the songs.
outro = And that's the Calendar Manager. Plan ahead, let the pre-service loop run itself, and plan together from anywhere.
