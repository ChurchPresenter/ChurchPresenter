intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll bring a service plan from Planning Center into ChurchPresenter, with its songs, scripture and files.
what = If your church plans its services in Planning Center, there's no need to type the order of service again. ChurchPresenter reads the plan and builds the schedule for you.
import-button = Under the schedule, click Import from Planning Center.
connect = The first time, ChurchPresenter asks you to connect. Click Connect to Planning Center.
browser = Your browser opens. Sign in to Planning Center, and allow ChurchPresenter to read your plans.
connected = Back in ChurchPresenter, the import window shows who's connected. ChurchPresenter only reads your plans. It never changes anything in Planning Center.
service-type = Choose the service type, like Sunday.
plan = Then pick the plan for this week.
items = Every item in the plan is listed, in the same order.
headers = Headers, like Worship and Message, come in as labels, so the schedule is divided the same way.
matched = Songs already in your library are marked Matched, and they're ticked.
add-song = A song you don't have yet shows Add Song. Click it, and the song editor opens with the lyrics from Planning Center, ready to save to your library.
scripture = When an item names a passage, like Psalm 23, ChurchPresenter finds it in your Bible and offers it as a reading.
announcement = Other items, like the welcome, can come in as announcements.
files = An item with files attached shows how many. Click it to see them, and tick the ones you want. Slides, pictures and videos all come in.
unsupported = Files ChurchPresenter can't show, like a Word document, are crossed out.
import = Click Import Selected.
schedule = And the whole plan is in your schedule: labels, songs, the reading, the announcement and the sermon slides, in the order you planned.
outro = And that's Planning Center: plan the service once, and bring it straight into ChurchPresenter.
