# What the narrator says in the q&a tour, in order. Edit freely: one line per key.
# Write it the way it should sound -- full sentences; numbers as words where it matters.

intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll look at the Q and A tab, where your congregation can send in questions from their phones during the service.
open-tab = Let's open the Q and A tab.
server = Q and A works through ChurchPresenter's companion server, so let's make sure it's switched on first. Open Settings, and go to the Server page.
server-enable = Turn on the Companion Server. This is what lets phones connect to ChurchPresenter. Once it's running, its status changes to Running.
sharing = Now click Sharing and Remote Access to see how people can reach your Q and A.
qr-scan = Everyone in the room can simply scan this QR code with their phone camera. It opens the question page right in their web browser, so there's no app to download.
admin = There's also an admin panel, so a volunteer can approve and manage the questions from their own phone or tablet.
wifi = The local address works for anyone whose phone is connected to the church WiFi.
public = But not everyone will be on your church's network. For those people, turn on Public Access. ChurchPresenter opens a secure tunnel, so phones can send questions over their own mobile data, without joining your WiFi.
cooldown = The cooldown sets how many seconds someone has to wait between questions, so one person can't flood the list.
qr-message = And you can change the message shown next to the QR code, such as, scan to ask a question.
show-qr = Back in the tab, this button puts the QR code on the big screen, so everyone can scan it from their seat.
voting = Turn on voting, and people can vote for the questions they most want answered. The vote count appears next to each question.
incoming = New questions appear here, with the name of the person who asked and how many votes it has.
approve = Click the tick to approve a question, so it's ready to be answered.
deny = Click the cross to deny a question you don't want to use.
edit = The pencil lets you edit a question, to fix a typo or tidy up the wording before it goes on screen.
delete = And the bin removes a question for good.
filter = Use Filter and Sort to show only the approved or denied questions, or to put the most popular ones at the top.
go-live = When you're ready to answer an approved question, click its Go Live button. Questions that haven't been approved yet ask you to confirm first, so nothing goes up by accident.
live = And there it is, the question on the big screen, ready to be answered.
clear = When it's been answered, click Clear Display to take it down.
history = Questions from earlier services are kept in History, so you can look back at them at any time.
new-session = At the start of each service, click New Session for a fresh list. The previous questions are saved to history.
add-own = You can also add your own questions. Type one in here.
add-click = Then click Add, and it joins the list just like the others.
outro = And that's the Q and A tab. Share the QR code, approve the best questions, and put them on screen with a single click.
