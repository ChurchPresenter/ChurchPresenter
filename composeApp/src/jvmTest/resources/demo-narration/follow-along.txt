intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, the Bible tab follows the sermon by itself. When the preacher names a verse or reads one aloud, ChurchPresenter finds it and puts it on the screen.
what = It listens to the same live captions as the S T T tab. A scripture engine inside ChurchPresenter reads every sentence as it arrives, looking for Bible references and for the words of the verses in your Bibles.
connect = First, connect the S T T tab to your speech-to-text server. The S T T tour shows how to set that up.
detect = Scripture detection is the Detect scripture switch, in the S T T Display Settings next to Connect. It's on from the start.
open-tab = Now open the Bible tab. While the captions are connected, a detection strip appears under the search box.
listening = On the left, the status says Listening once the engine is running and the captions are coming in.
spoken = The preacher says, turn with me to John chapter fifteen, verse one.
item = The reference appears in the list, with the start of the verse beside it.
badges = The icons in front of it say how it was found. This one was a spoken reference, heard in the transcription.
version = On the right is the translation the preacher seems to be reading from, matched against the Bibles in your folder in the same language. The words on screen still come from your own Bible.
click = Click a detected reference to open that verse in the Bible tab, ready to go.
double = Double-click it to put it straight on the screen.
auto = To let ChurchPresenter keep up by itself, turn on Auto-follow.
follow = Now, as the preacher reads on, it follows along verse by verse, and each verse goes to the screen as it's read.
speed = Next verse speed sets how soon it moves on while a passage is being read. Balanced waits for more of each verse. Fast moves on sooner, but may jump ahead now and then.
text-match = Text match finds verses that are quoted without a reference. Click it to choose how eager it is: Conservative, Balanced or Aggressive. Off turns it off.
clear-screen = Let's clear the screen for a moment, to see what happens next.
quote = The preacher quotes, for God so loved the world, without saying where it's from.
staged = It's matched by its words. A match like that doesn't go to the screen by itself. It opens in the Bible tab and waits for you.
tiers = Only clear matches go straight to the screen: a spoken reference, the next verse of a passage being read, or a verse found in the chapter you're in.
send = If it's the right verse, double-click it to show it.
clear-list = To start fresh, the X button clears the list.
other-tab = Auto-follow keeps working on any other tab, as long as the Bible is on the screen.
other-verse = Here we're on the Songs tab, and the preacher moves on to verse five. It's on the screen right away.
tips = For the best results, use a microphone close to the speaker, and install the translation your preacher reads from.
outro = And that's scripture follow-along: every verse on the screen as soon as it's read.
