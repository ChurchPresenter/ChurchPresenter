intro = Welcome to ChurchPresenter. In this tour, we'll use the mobile app on its own, with your phone as the presenter and no computer at all.
why = It's made for a small group, a youth night, a home church or a service outdoors, and it's a handy backup when the computer won't start.
mode = The first time you open the app, it asks how you'll present. Choose Standalone, and tap Continue.
tabs = Across the bottom are the app's tabs: Present, Songs, Bible, Library and More.
settings = Settings is behind the gear at the top right of every tab.
settings-mode = Under Mode, you can switch between Standalone, Remote control and Calendar at any time.
settings-computer = Under Computer, you set the computer your songs and Bibles are copied from.
settings-appearance = And under Appearance, you choose a system, light or dark theme for the app itself.
library = The Library holds your songs and announcements. They're kept on the phone, so they work with no computer and no internet.
sync = To bring your songs over from the computer, tap Sync from Computer. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi, with the Companion Server switched on in ChurchPresenter.
sync-books = Copy all your songbooks, or choose just the ones you need. Songs you've edited on the phone are never overwritten.
write = You can also write your own songs and announcements right on the phone, with the Add button.
bibles = The phone keeps its own Bibles too, and there are two ways to get one.
bible-computer = The first is to copy a translation from your computer. Tap See What Your Computer Has, and pick the translations you want. Each one is around five megabytes.
bible-web = The second is to download one from the internet. Tap Get Bibles to open the catalog, with translations from eBible.org, Zefania and Holy Bible XML.
bible-filter = Narrow the list down by language or by source, and tap Get on the translation you want.
licence = Before anything downloads, the app shows that translation's copyright and license. Many modern translations can't be shown in public without permission, so only download and project Bibles your church is licensed to use. The same goes for any Bible you copy from your computer.
bible-ready = The phone downloads the translation and converts it, and then it's ready to read and present.
screens = Next, connect a screen. Tap the screen button at the top of the Present tab to see every screen, and whether it's connected.
ios = On an iPhone or iPad, open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your Apple TV. A USB-C or Lightning to HDMI adapter works too.
android = On Android, swipe down from the top of the screen, tap Cast, and pick your Chromecast. An HDMI or USB-C adapter works too.
web = Or use the Web page screen. Any TV, laptop or projector with a web browser can open the address it shows, and you can scan its QR code to open it on another device.
stays = However you connect, the slides go to the screen, and the controls stay on your phone. Keep the app open and the phone's screen on while you present.
song = Now let's present. In the Songs tab, open Amazing Grace, and tap the present button at the bottom right.
present = The song loads in the Present tab. The preview shows the slide, with every verse listed below it.
live = Tap Live to put it on the screen.
next = Next and Prev move through the verses, and the list shows which one is on the screen.
blank = Blank hides the words for a moment, and Clear takes everything off the screen.
bible-present = Bible verses work the same way. Open a chapter, pick the verses, and tap present.
look = To change how your slides look, tap Look.
look-themes = Start from a theme, like Black, Paper or Midnight, or set your own gradient, text color, size and font.
saved-looks = When you like a look, save it, and it's ready to use again next week.
backdrop = Behind the words, the backdrop can be a gradient, plain black, or one of your own photos.
announcements = Announcements from your library go live from the More tab, with one tap.
outro = And that's standalone: your whole service, from one phone, with no computer needed.
